	Sumaya Hirsi

Description:
	BEETLEJUS is a short game inspired by Annapurna Interactive's Storyteller. It follows the story
	of a troubled Beetlejus who, after getting a bad grade, gets evicted from his home. The player must 
	figure out how to piece different aspects of the story (backdrops, characters, objects) in the correct
	order to reveal the details of the story.
	
Controls:
	Only a mouse/trackpad is required to play BEETLEJUS. 

Find out how Beetlejuice gets evicted by piecing together the story yourself!

How we made it:
This game was created using Godot's GDScript for the Cozy Fall Jam 2026.
